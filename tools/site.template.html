Why we built this

Your accounts are set to collect as much as possible by default. Privacy Sweep is one way to take some control back: free, fast, open source, and it can't see your results. No account, no analytics, everything stays in your browser.

It's a cleanup. The bigger fix is a web where you don't have to hand your data over to begin with: you prove what's needed ("I'm over 18", "I can afford this") and keep the rest. That's what we're building at Midnight.
