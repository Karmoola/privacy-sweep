Wait, how do I know you're not watching?

Fair question. When you click a link, Google (or Meta, or Amazon) opens in its own tab and you log in there. Browsers don't let one website peek into another, so this page never sees what you type or what's on your screen.

On top of that, this page is locked so it can't send anything anywhere. Not to us, not to anyone. If you read code, view the source and look for connect-src.

If you don't, try this: save the page, turn off your Wi-Fi, open it again. Still works. There's nobody on the other end.

Your ticks are kept in your own browser so you can finish later. "Start over" wipes them. The receipt only shows setting names and numbers, never your name or email, and it stays on your computer unless you post it.

AI agents are a different deal. They read the pages they work on and send that to whichever AI company runs them. You still type your own passwords, but if that bugs you, stick with this page.

Made by people building Midnight. Settings move around a lot, so if one's wrong, tell us on GitHub. This only flips switches the companies already give you; whether they honour them is on them.
